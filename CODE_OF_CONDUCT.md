# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, respectful, and focused
on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Assume privacy and safety constraints matter.
- Critique code, design, and tradeoffs rather than people.
- Keep reports and examples free of private user data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information, including mail content or
  addresses, without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews, or discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may
restrict further participation. To report a problem privately, contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile.
