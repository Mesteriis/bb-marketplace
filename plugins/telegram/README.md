# Makosh Telegram

Telegram follows BB’s appearance settings. Light and dark mode, the active
palette, and its interface font apply to the chat list, conversation, composer,
and Telegram dialogs. Chat wallpapers and media keep their own artwork.

The Telegram interface supports English and Russian. In BB’s Telegram plugin
settings, **Telegram interface language** offers **System**, **Русский**, and
**English**. System mode follows BB’s Russian shell or the first supported
device language, with English as the fallback. Telegram messages, chat names,
captions, file names, and links keep their original text.

On the desktop Telegram page, folders and chats use BB’s left sidebar. The
conversation stays in the center and **Chat details** uses BB’s right side
panel. Split and compact views keep navigation inside Telegram and open chat
details in a docked column on the right.
**Shared media** retracts chat details and opens the gallery in the center.
Going back resumes the conversation with the draft and reading position intact.

Open **Chat details → Actions** to translate the chat or select messages.
Use the message’s **…** button or right-click it for message actions, and use
**ⓘ** for its information. During multi-selection, the bottom bar shows the
count and available bulk actions. Open its **…** menu to copy text, export
attachments, or report the selection when available. Press **Esc** or use the
close button to return to the draft.

Right-click a whole album for album actions or a photo or video tile for that
item's actions. Chat rows, folders, and active controls offer their available
actions. The Telegram pane leaves empty space free of context menus. Forwarding
an item or selection opens a separate recipient dialog without moving the
conversation. Use Enter in **Find recipient** to search and Escape to close.

Select draft text to open formatting by the composer, or use **Aa** to open
it manually. **…** in that panel opens additional styles such as spoilers and
quotes. The paperclip opens attachments, Telegram draft actions, and the
available bot commands, mini apps, message effects and quick replies. Typing
**/** shows matching quick replies as you type.

A forwarded photo and video album from the same source can share a single
header and caption. The **…** menu on each tile still opens that item's
original post.

In **Settings → Business away message**, choose a quick reply and recipients.
Telegram can send it at any time, outside Business opening hours, or during a
custom period. You can require at least 10 minutes offline and disable the
auto-reply from the same settings.

In **Settings → Business bot**, find a compatible bot by username and review
its private-chat scope, exclusions, and permissions before connecting it.
The same section can disconnect the current bot. In a private chat’s details,
**Business bot access** can pause, resume or remove the bot for that chat.
When Telegram reports a pending Business bot connection from another device,
the settings show the requested access and available connection details.
Choose **Review pending connection** to confirm it or **Disconnect bot** to
remove it from your account.

Open **Settings → Telegram Business → Business chat links** to load your links
and see their view counts. Create or edit a link with an optional title and
prepared message, copy its URL to share it, or confirm deletion of an old link.
The prepared message can include formatting and links.
Follow a Business link from a message or paste it into chat search and choose
**Open Business chat**. In the destination, **Insert link draft** adds the
prepared message to the composer. If there is a Telegram draft, load it first
to keep its text and formatting. The combined draft is ready for manual review
and sending.

With Telegram Business, open **Settings → Business greeting** to choose a
quick reply, inactivity period, and eligible private chats. Telegram sends
the greeting automatically once the setting is saved.

With Telegram Business, open **Settings → Business hours** to select a time
zone and save weekly opening periods. Select the following day as the closing
day for overnight periods. To remove published hours, clear all periods and save.

With Telegram Business, open **Settings → Business address** to save an
address with or without map coordinates. Choose **Remove address** and save
to clear it from Telegram.

With Telegram Business, open **Settings → Business start page** to edit the
title and message shown before a conversation starts. Use **Choose greeting
sticker** to select one from an installed pack or **Remove greeting sticker**
to clear it. Save the start page to apply the change.

Open **Settings → Telegram Business → Load quick replies** to see your
existing shortcuts, message counts, and first-message text previews.
Select **Show messages** to read the shortcut in order. The view renders
formatted text and captions, opens photos, and plays supported media whose size Telegram knows. Static and animated stickers load when visible. Use **Download** to save available documents through BB.
If Telegram failed to add a message to an existing shortcut, **Retry adding
message** appears when Telegram reports that the message can be retried.
Open shortcuts briefly refresh pending messages automatically. Use **Refresh
message status** to check an upload that takes longer.
Captions appear above or below the media according to their position in Telegram.
Existing albums group consecutive items and keep the actions for each message.
An album with one caption shows it above or below the whole album.
Shared contacts show their name and phone number. Locations and venues show
coordinates and any available venue name and address. Other attachments are
identified by type.
Click a photo to enlarge it. Photo spoilers require a click to reveal.
Choose **Edit message** to change text and formatting on a supported,
editable shortcut message.
Use **Edit photo caption** on a photo or **Edit caption** on a supported
document, video, GIF, audio file or voice note to change its caption while
keeping the same attachment.
For photos, videos and GIFs, the caption editor also lets you change its position
and turn the media spoiler on or off.
If Telegram changes the message while you edit it, copy your draft from the
editor before reloading the current shortcut.
Use **Delete message** to remove an individual message. Removing the last
message removes its shortcut in Telegram.
Use **Rename** to change an existing shortcut name. If the shortcut changed
in Telegram, refresh the list and review its current name before saving.
Choose **Shortcut document** to attach a file to a new or existing shortcut.
Use **Choose shortcut sticker** to add a sticker from an installed pack.
Choose **Upload as video** for MP4 or MOV, **Upload as audio** for MP3 or AAC in M4A, **Upload as GIF** for a GIF animation, or **Upload as voice message** for mono Ogg Opus.
Choose **Upload as video note** for an MP4 or WebM clip up to 8 MiB. BB uses
FFmpeg to crop it to a square and limit it to 60 seconds.
Files use BB's streamed upload and the Telegram account's attachment limit.
Use **New quick reply** to create a shortcut with its first text message, or
**Add message** to append text to an existing shortcut. Refresh the list if
Telegram is still processing the update.
Select text and choose **Format message** to add formatting or a link to a
shortcut message or attachment caption before saving.
Choose **Shortcut photo or album** to add one photo or an album of two to ten
photos and MP4 or MOV videos. Videos in an album upload in chunks. Use
**Move up**, **Move down**, or **Remove** to review the album before saving.
The album can have a caption.
For a photo, video, GIF or album, you can hide the media behind spoilers and place the caption above it.
Choose **Add contact card** to save a phone contact as a shortcut message.
Choose **Add location** to save a static point by latitude and longitude.
Fill in both the place name and address to save a venue card.
Use **Delete** in settings to review the shortcut and its message count
before confirming removal.
Use **Move up** or **Move down** to save the shortcut order in Telegram.
In group or channel **Chat details → Welcome messages**, eligible
administrators can load the list and add or edit text greetings using the
formatting controls and links. Editing is available when BB can preserve the
existing formatting. Select an optional photo to create a photo greeting with
or without a caption. Existing supported photos open in the photo viewer.
When editing a photo greeting, you can update the caption or replace the
photo. The section also lets you add and edit a contact card, a coordinate
pin, or a named place with an address. Cards with linked contact or venue
data are available for review. Choose a sticker from an installed set and
save to add or replace a sticker greeting. WebP, TGS and WebM artwork loads
when the greeting is visible. **Add welcome GIF** opens the saved GIF
collection. A GIF greeting can be replaced or its caption edited. Use the
player for supported GIF, audio, voice and video greetings. You can edit
captions on supported documents, audio, videos and voice notes while keeping
the existing files. **Add welcome attachment** streams documents, videos,
audio, GIFs, voice notes and video notes from local files. Eligible existing
attachments can be replaced with the same media type. BB shows local transfer
progress and accepts cancellation until dispatch to TDLib. Use **Download**
to save a document. **Add formatted welcome** opens the block editor for
paragraphs, headings, quotes, code, lists and tables on eligible accounts.
New greetings can include photo, video and file blocks in the same message.
Existing structured greetings can be edited when BB can preserve every block.
The editor can update text, move or remove blocks, add media, and replace
supported photos, videos and documents with files of the same type. Supported
embedded captions can be edited or cleared, including during file replacement.
Caption edits retain credit and spoiler settings. Embedded photos, playable
media and documents in existing greetings can be viewed or downloaded.
Select an individual greeting and confirm to delete it.

With Telegram Business, open **Quick replies** next to the composer in a
private chat and select **Send** for a shortcut. Telegram sends the messages
in that shortcut to the open chat.
Type **/** or the beginning of a shortcut name in an eligible private chat
to filter the list. **Arrow Down** or **Enter** in the input focuses the first
match. Activate a shortcut to send it, or press **Escape** to close the list
and keep the draft.
Select **Edit /shortcut** to open its messages in Telegram Business settings.
The chat draft stays in the composer when you return.
BB checks the shortcut name, message count and displayed preview again before sending.
If part of the shortcut is still pending or has failed to upload, BB asks you
to check the shortcut in Telegram settings before sending.
If BB cannot confirm that Telegram accepted the entire shortcut, check the chat
before retrying.
In Telegram settings, use **Quick replies → Add poll** while creating or
adding to a shortcut. Save an anonymous text poll or quiz, then open the
shortcut's messages to review its question and answers.

Open **Gifts** in a personal or channel profile to browse gifts visible there.
Artwork appears when available. TGS gift animations play while visible and respect the reduced-motion setting. Choose a collection to filter the list. Collectible gifts link to their
Telegram page. Expand **Collectible details** to see attributes, rarity and
minted supply when available. Collectible links in conversations and Instant View open the gift card inside BB. In Saved Messages, use **Gifts** in the chat details to create
a collection or rename, reorder, or delete one in your own profile.
Use **Edit gift collections** on a gift to add or remove it from a collection.
Channel owners and administrators with posting rights can manage their channel’s collections.
**Browse gift catalog** shows Telegram’s gift prices, limited quantities and resale summaries. Resale listings can be filtered by model, symbol and backdrop.
Use **Check availability** to refresh a price and check known recipient and catalog restrictions.
**Review gift send** shows the recipient, message, sender visibility and total
Stars cost. Your name and message are visible only to the recipient by default.
On a profile you manage, use **Hide from profile** to remove a received gift
from public view. Enable **Include hidden gifts** to find it and show it again.
Use **Pin gift** or **Unpin gift** on a visible collectible to change which
gifts appear at the top of the profile.
If you have posting rights in a channel, **Notify me about new gifts**
controls whether Telegram sends you notifications for that channel's gifts.
**Review conversion to Stars** shows the current amount for an eligible gift.
Confirming the conversion permanently removes the gift.
**Review gift upgrade** shows the current Stars cost or prepayment and any
example models Telegram returns. You can choose whether to keep the original
sender, recipient and message before confirming the upgrade.
**Review gift transfer** lets you find a person or channel and confirm the
current Stars fee for a transferable collectible.
**Review removal of gift details** shows the fee and asks for confirmation
before permanently removing the original sender, recipient and message.
**Manage gift resale** lists an eligible personal collectible in Stars or
TON, changes its price, or takes it off sale after review.
Under **Settings → Profile → Receiving gifts**, choose whether to always show
the gift button. Telegram Premium accounts can also choose which gift types to accept.
If the gift supports an upgrade, you can prepay it for the recipient.
Use **Browse resale listings** to browse collectible offers, sort them, or filter to Stars.
Choose a payment currency and confirm the recipient and exact resale price before buying.
**View auction** shows the current round, minimum bid, and auction results.
For an eligible personal recipient, enter a Stars bid and confirm the amount and recipient.
A changed quote requires a new confirmation. Check Stars activity if Telegram did not confirm delivery.

Main and named Telegram Mini App links can open after confirmation inside BB, including native Telegram deep links.
Mini Apps can store up to ten secure values per bot in macOS Keychain.
Secure values are isolated by BB profile and Telegram account as well as bot.
They remain available across BB restarts. If Keychain is locked or unavailable,
the Mini App receives a storage error.

When opening a Mini App in a compatible secure BB browser, select **Allow camera**
or **Allow microphone** to enable the device. BB remembers the choice in this browser
for the current Telegram account, the app entry point, and the approved origin.
The **Camera and microphone** button in the Mini App header changes the selection
and reloads the frame. BB asks again if the launch origin changes. Telegram's
installation, terms and write-access prompts retain their own confirmation.
The browser may also prompt for permission.


In a bot chat, the grid icon in the header opens the bot’s configured menu app, or its main app if it has no menu app. A bot profile with a main app also offers **Open bot app**. When the main app opened from the bot chat or profile requires origin-checked events, BB runs it in an isolated frame on browsers with credentialless iframe support.
A Mini App starts in a modal centered over the full BB page with a blurred backdrop.
Use **Open Mini App in split view** in the title bar to keep the conversation and app visible at the same time. Telegram reserves a pane on the right, or below the conversation when its panel is narrow. Switching between the modal and split keeps the current Mini App session and page. Agent access opens as a bottom sheet when the Mini App pane is narrow.
Use **WebApp event console** in the window menu to inspect Telegram bridge events for the current app. The console logs event names and frame lifecycle locally. For JavaScript logs and execution, select the app frame by origin in the browser’s DevTools console.
Choose **Agent access** in the window header and select a BB thread to share the app's visible controls for the current session. Navigation access additionally lets that thread use the Mini App's Telegram Back control when the app exposes it. The activity log shows access changes, tool inspections, and whether BB sent the Back event. The tool does not read the third-party page or click arbitrary buttons. Access expires when the app stops checking in and can be revoked immediately from the panel.

Mini App buttons and bot menu entries in private chats open with consent.
For reply-keyboard Mini Apps, review the requested data before returning it to the bot.
The **Attachment Mini Apps** button in the composer lists installed apps that Telegram allows in the current chat or forum topic. The launch dialog identifies where the app can return a message.
An attachment Mini App link for the current chat opens a destination review. If installation is needed, the review requests acceptance of the Mini App terms and shows any messaging permission the bot requests.
Links that ask you to choose a chat offer recent eligible chats and search before the destination review.
Links with a fixed Telegram username or phone number show the resolved chat in the same launch review.
**Mini Apps** in the Telegram sidebar lists installed side-menu apps. BB can open them independently of the active conversation.
Use **Manage installed apps** there to review apps installed in either Telegram menu and confirm removal.
The isolated frame supports viewport, theme, closing confirmation, and main, secondary and back button events.
Mini Apps can show popups, expand the window and request full screen. Entering full screen requires a user action.
On browsers with the corresponding hardware APIs, a focused Mini App can
hide its on-screen keyboard and give haptic feedback during an interaction.
A Mini App can lock the current orientation in its own full-screen window.
Use **Minimize Mini App** to return to the chat while keeping the app open.
When multiple apps are minimized, **Minimized Mini Apps** opens a compact
list of their windows. Press **Escape** to dismiss the list.
Open the same Telegram Mini App link again to resume its existing window.
Links with different start parameters or window modes open separately.
Selecting a minimized app from the same bot menu, bot profile or Telegram
sidebar also resumes its window. A changed bot menu opens a separate review.
Use **Reload Mini App** to load its launch page again in the isolated frame
while keeping the current Telegram session. Reload becomes available after
pending requests finish. BB asks before reloading if the app requested
protection for unsaved changes.
In full screen, the app fills the space between the header and any visible controls. An exit request cancels entry while the browser is still switching to full screen.
When a Mini App asks to scan a QR code, **Use camera** opens the camera in BB.
BB decodes frames locally, including in browsers without a built-in QR
detector, and sends the recognized text to the requesting app. Closing the
scanner stops the camera. Use **Scan QR from image** to read a PNG, JPEG or
WebP locally without opening the camera. The app receives the QR text.
When a Mini App asks to let its bot message you, BB shows a confirmation before granting new access.
For file download requests, BB checks the request with Telegram and shows the
bot, filename, and source host. **Download file** passes the URL to the browser,
which handles the response from the remote server. On supported browsers,
**Save file as…** proposes the filename supplied by the bot and streams the
response into the file you choose. If the server blocks direct access,
**Download file** remains available.
An open Mini App can invoke its own bot's custom Telegram methods through BB.
When a Mini App offers a prepared message, BB shows the result metadata
available from Telegram, such as a link, address, thumbnail, or file
information. Choose a chat or an open forum topic, then confirm the send.
When a Mini App requests a group, forum or channel, you can create one from the
request. If the bot requires a public chat, choose its public username. When
a username is taken during creation, retry with a different username on the
same chat. BB verifies the new chat against the request and shows it in the
selection. Choose **Confirm sharing** to send the selection to the bot.
Mini App buttons support custom emoji icons and respect reduced-motion
preferences for the shine effect.
Mini Apps receive Telegram's desktop fallback statuses for motion sensors, biometric authentication and home-screen shortcuts.
When it requests your phone number, BB names the bot. **Share phone number** sends your own contact card to that bot in Telegram.
Mini App links are reviewed before opening. For a public Mini App or the
main app from a bot’s public profile, use **Open Mini App in Telegram** to
continue in the system Telegram app when device access is needed. Apps can show a **Mini App settings** button and set colors for their header, frame background, bottom bar and action buttons. Supported Telegram links can open inside BB. A Mini App can request a fresh
sticker pack from Telegram when you approve opening the pack link. Apps that require
their own origin or other Telegram Web App capabilities may need to open in Telegram.

A public Telegram link with the `profile` parameter opens the contact or chat
profile. Open it in a message or paste it into All Chats and select the result.
An ordinary temporary Telegram contact link opens the contact profile from a
message. In All Chats, paste the link and select **Open temporary profile**.
A link that Telegram has expired appears as unavailable.
In Telegram settings, open **Profile** and select **Generate temporary profile
link** to copy a link to your own profile or display its QR code. BB shows the
expiry time and removes the copy and QR controls when the link expires.
A public link with `text` offers the prepared text in that chat. Use **Insert
link draft** to add it to the composer and preserve any existing draft.

Open a Telegram **Share** link in a message or paste it into All Chats. Choose a
conversation from All Chats or use **Recipient folder** to browse a folder.
Use **Insert shared link** to append the URL and optional text
to its composer. If the conversation has a Telegram draft, load that draft first
to keep its text. Use the composer’s send button to send the finished draft.
For a forum, open **Topics** and select the destination topic before inserting.

Confirmed text sent from the composer animates into its bubble at the
current end of a chat, forum topic or channel discussion. BB respects the
system Reduce Motion setting and keeps the current history position.

Use **Instant View** below an eligible Telegram link preview to open the article
in BB. The article stays open if the conversation changes, its preview leaves
the timeline, or resizing the window switches between panel layouts.
The reader preserves article subtitles and section heading levels and shows
authors, publication dates, and attributed or expandable quotes. Nested
quotes and code retain their structure and code keeps its language label
and copy control. The reader also supports dividers, inline and displayed
formulas, inline formatting, and web links. Bank-card, TON-address and
bot-command labels retain their formatting in web articles. In formatted
Telegram messages, selecting a bot command sends it in the source chat and
topic. A command hidden by a spoiler appears after the spoiler is revealed.
Selecting a bank card in a formatted message shows a copy action and
requests issuer information from Telegram. Available issuer links open in
the browser. Selecting a valid TON address in a formatted message
opens Telegram Wallet in the system Telegram app with the recipient filled
in. Enter the amount and confirm the transfer there.
Links to content inside a collapsed section open that section. Links to anchors
at the end of a section or article scroll to that position. Footnote links
also open the referenced passage inside the reader. Use **Back to previous
section** to return to the link and resume reading. Inline article images
support JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP. Open an unspoiled article photo, including
a gallery item, and select **Save photo** or press **⌘/Ctrl+S** to download
the largest supported Telegram file for that image. Video and GIF preview frames reserve space
at the aspect ratio supplied by Telegram. Music in articles and their galleries shows the
track title, performer and available cover. Playback publishes the title and
performer to the system media controls when supported. **Copy table** uses tabs between columns,
keeps empty positions for merged or invisible cells, and conceals spoilers.
Lists in Instant View and formatted messages preserve each item's supplied
numbering, label or checkbox, including lists with mixed markers.
Article buttons support web and Telegram links, opening a Telegram user profile,
and copying text after a click. Unsupported inline actions retain their labels
without an active control.
Related articles supplied by Telegram show available cover thumbnails and open in Instant View when available. Supported Telegram deep links in article text, link buttons and photo destinations open in BB. Select a linked photo to follow its destination, or use **Enlarge Telegram photo** to inspect the image.
On a public channel or group card in an article, use **Review join** to
see the current chat name and whether it requires approval. Confirm
membership or send the request while keeping the article open.
Use the photo viewer's arrows to move between photos in a collage or slideshow. Repeated photos keep their own positions and captions.
Galleries, nested lists and quotes can contain more than 20 blocks. They share the reader's
200-block limit with the rest of the article. Links to the end of a slideshow select its final slide.

In the chat photo viewer, swipe horizontally at fit size to move between
loaded photos. Pinch on a touchscreen or use a trackpad pinch to zoom in and
out. Drag a zoomed photo with one finger to pan it.
For a photo you can save, choose **Prepare photo to drag** and drag
**Drag photo out** to a file drop target in a compatible browser. BB prepares
an expiring one-use download for the selected photo. **Save photo** is also
available from the same viewer.
For chat photos that can be saved, **Recognize text** uses Vision on the Mac
running the BB server. Select the result or use **Copy recognized text**.
Click a highlighted line on the photo to select that line in the text field.
This feature requires Swift from Xcode or the Apple Command Line Tools.
If Telegram returns an excerpt, **Retry full article** requests the full version while keeping the excerpt open.
When the full article adds content above your reading position, BB keeps the
same visible paragraph or other matching text block in view.
Each article retries independently. If you follow another Instant View link
while a request is pending, its result is available when you go back.
When Telegram provides a feedback link, **Give Instant View feedback** opens the bot's review flow.
When Telegram includes revisions, **Show changes** displays before and after excerpts with spoilers concealed.

In a Telegram game message, select its play button, then choose **Play game from** the host shown by BB. The game opens in an isolated viewer, which also offers **Open game in browser**.
Game messages can show a cover, a playable animation and the formatted score text supplied by the bot. Select a score notice in chat history to open the game message it references.
In the game viewer, select **Share game** or use the game's share control to open BB's forwarding review. Choose a Telegram chat and, for a forum, a topic before selecting **Forward**.
Paste a Telegram game link into **All chats**, or open it from a message, to view the game and its bot. Choose a private chat, group or forum topic and select **Send game** to put the playable game into that conversation.
Public chat and channel links in article cards open the conversation in BB.
Maps in articles, including collages and slideshows, show a preview on request and can open in OpenStreetMap.
Embedded posts show their author, date, content, caption and source when available.
External embeds show available poster images and captions and link to the source.
After you load an external embed, BB adjusts its frame height when the page reports a new size.
Use **Show embedded preview** to read the supplied HTML as static content,
including when the embed also offers a link to load the external page.
Article chat cards and embedded posts load available avatars when visible.
Use **Download** on an article attachment to save it through BB.
If Telegram has yet to report the exact size of a video or audio clip,
open the player to check the size. When playback requires a complete
download to determine the size, BB supports files up to 32 MiB. Clips with
an exact size use the usual streaming path.
Article players show the duration supplied by Telegram. Voice notes with a
waveform show the same seek control as voice notes in chats.
With focus in a player that can download its original, **⌘S** or **Ctrl+S**
saves the original. This also works for playable attachments in chats.
Article buttons can open links or copy text on request.
Use **Back to article** to return to your prior reading position or
**Open original** to open the current page in the browser.
Use **Share article** to choose a chat or forum topic, then **Insert shared
link** to add the current article's URL to the destination draft.
Use **Zoom** or **Cmd/Ctrl +**, **Cmd/Ctrl -**, and **Cmd/Ctrl 0** to resize
the article, reset to 100%, and keep your place as the content reflows.
Visible article photos load a sharper version when their on-screen size
exceeds the preview resolution.
Links to supported sections move within the open article. Nested lists retain
their numbering and checkbox states. Wide tables scroll
horizontally. Cover photos, collages, and slideshows load as they come into view.
Use the previous and next controls or arrow keys on those controls to navigate an article slideshow.
The article shows photo captions and credits. Click a photo to enlarge it and reveal spoilers
manually. **Open original** opens the full web page.
Captions and credits remain visible when an article or gallery file is unavailable.
Unavailable photos retain their link destination, and unavailable maps retain their captions.
If streamed media fails to open, use **Retry playback** from the article.
MP4 and WebM videos with a known size stream on request and support seeking
in the article, collages, and slideshows.
Video and GIF previews supplied by Telegram load when visible. Reveal a spoiler
to show its preview and playback control.
MP4 animations loop after opening. GIFs play in the reader and use the
media playback size limit.
Standalone audio and voice notes with a supported MIME type and a known
size open in the same player on request. FLAC also plays in article galleries
and in profile music when Telegram reports its size.

In chat history, supported audio files, including FLAC, stream on request
when Telegram reports their exact size. The player can seek without first
downloading the whole file.
If playback fails, **Retry playback** reloads that attachment and restores
the position when the player had already read the media duration.
Use **Download** beside unspoiled videos, GIFs, audio, and voice notes to save
their original files, including in collages and slideshows.

Select **Unread** above the chat list to show chats with unread messages or a manual unread mark in the current folder, archive, or main list.
Use **Mark all chats as read** to update the entire selected list, including unread forum topics and chats on later pages.

Reposts show attribution and let you open the original when Telegram supplies a public story reference. Story link areas open the marked destination after you select them. Choose or remove an emoji reaction in the viewer. Select a suggested reaction on the story to apply or remove it. Supported custom emoji and their counts appear on the story. MAKOSH_TELEGRAM_API_ID=<app ID>

```sh
call_runtime="$HOME/Library/Application Support/Makosh/telegram-calls"
uv venv --python 3.13 "$call_runtime"
printf '%s\n' 'ntgcalls==3.0.7 --hash=sha256:042f613a2d16fe3bb3b0f7c57067145f70d2396eebeeb42dbf71cd17133600b1' \
  'pillow==13.0.0 --hash=sha256:f11a3c1358bb3c68012a7b1f41b223c3db9f7bfde90b481df028cfe1c3363313' |
  uv pip install --python "$call_runtime/bin/python" --no-deps --require-hashes -r -
```
