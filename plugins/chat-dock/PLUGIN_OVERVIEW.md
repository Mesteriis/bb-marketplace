BB Chat Dock adds a **ChatGPT** tab to BB with chatgpt.com in it. Sign in
once and chat right in the tab. Nothing else: the plugin does not read your
conversations, insert text or call ChatGPT's API.

## What you get

- A **ChatGPT** tab in the sidebar. The whole tab is ChatGPT: no address
  bar, buttons or other panels.
- Your sign-in persists across restarts and is kept apart from BB's Browser
  and its agent tools.

## Limits you should know

- chatgpt.com cannot be framed, so the tab is covered by a borderless window
  of the plugin's own that follows BB's window. BB Desktop only; BB menus
  that open over the tab appear under it.
- macOS 14+ with Xcode or the Command Line Tools: the window is compiled on
  your Mac the first time you open the tab.

No settings, CLI commands, agent tools or storage.
