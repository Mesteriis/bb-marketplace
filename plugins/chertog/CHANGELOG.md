# Changelog

## Unreleased

- A reloaded or restarted window takes over the thread panels of its previous document without a click: a window that shows a thread tile claims it when the owning window is no longer present (left, stopped answering, or crashed). A panel of a live window still needs the handoff. A receiver window that leaves or reloads still returns the panel to its source. Stand probe: `tests/adapters/app/window-reload.browser.mjs`.
- Settings «Тайловое пространство» (`tileSpace`), «Навигация» (`navigationPart`) and «Список тредов» (`threadListPart`). With the tile space off the window mounts no live layer, overlay, `chertog.open.v1` listener or thread subscription, the route shows one line, the title bar shows only the name, and the Home redirect does not fire; saved layouts return when it is on again (thread tiles as they were; embedded views such as Telegram, Zoom and SSH/RDP/VNC come back as a «Повторить» card and reopen on click). The two part switches enable and disable `makosh-navigation` and `makosh-sidebar` through `bb.sdk.plugins`, only when the switch itself changes (not at start, not for other keys), so a plugin disabled by hand is never re-enabled.
- Workspace controls moved into BB's title bar (focused tile and its menu on the left; layouts, "+ Панель", "Скрытые панели", "Новый тред" on the right, folded into "Ещё" when the title bar is narrower than 880 px). Tiles get a 24 px bar with "+ ⋯ ▢ ✕", a 6 px gap, rounded corners and a primary-colour border on the focused tile; the thread handoff moved from a row over the thread into the tile menu.
- Click in the thread list opens a tile (Makosh Sidebar sends `chertog.open.v1`), subthreads open as linked blocks beside their parent, tiles have "Открыть рядом…", the parent's keyboard move and close take the block along, and a window without a shown Chertog page answers open requests `not-shown`. Stand record: docs/acceptance-open.md.
- Stage A in one window. The "Чертог" page shows the tile surface on the whole route with BB's thread list beside it: existing threads in tiles and tabs, split, drag, resize, maximize, keyboard equivalents, and named layouts created, renamed, duplicated, deleted and switched from the toolbar.
- Live panels are kept for the life of the window in an app overlay, because BB remounts a nav panel on every return to its route. Leaving for a thread and coming back shows the same panels.
- Layouts are saved through the plugin's RPC after every change, one save at a time, against the revision they were made from. A conflict with another window is merged and reported; a failed save is shown and keeps the window's state.
- Removing a panel from the layout hides it and keeps it running, closing releases it and asks first when that ends a session, and a session is ended only by its own command.
- Commands: "Чертог: перейти к вводу выбранного тайла" and "Чертог: завершить сессию выбранной панели".
- The Home redirect section and the acceptance test panel are wired to the settings `homeRedirect` and `acceptanceFixture`, which the server declares.
- Package scaffold: manifest, the host-agnostic `src/core` and `src/ui` layers with their import check, the BB adapter layer, and the entry points.
