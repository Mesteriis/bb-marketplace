Chertog (Чертог) is planned as a tiling workspace for BB's Home: several
threads and tools side by side in named layouts that keep their panels alive
while you rearrange them.

## What you get today

Nothing visible. This version is the package scaffold: the plugin installs and
loads in BB, and adds no page, panel, command, setting, agent tool or CLI.
Home, threads and every other BB surface stay exactly as they were.

## Limits

- No workspace, tiles or layouts yet.
- Nothing is stored and nothing is sent anywhere.
